# The Four Pillars of Pyrognosis

**Critique - Fuel** 
Fire gives light before it gives comfort. A flame that fears examination is only smoke.

**Mindfulness — Heat**  
Fire does not dwell on how it was sparked or when it will go out. While it burns, it burns brightly.

**Flexibility— Oxygen**  
Fire does not command the wind; it dances with it, using the air to burn brighter.

**Freedom — The Chain Reaction**
Fire burns according to its own nature. Do not let others decide what your flame should be.

Fire knows what it wants. It knows what it is. It knows what it can be.

Fire knows without others to provide fuel, it will die out. 

Fire knows other flames can help it burn brighter.

Fire knows obsession with burning brighter will use up all it's fuel.

For a flame to grow and consume all. Eventually all fuel will disappear and never return.

For a flame to use the fuel it needs and share with other flames allow them all to grow brighter.

A bonfire that does not serves the flames will not grow brighter.

No flame is beyond question, not even Pyrognosis itself. A fire that cannot change is already ash.

Let every belief be tested by survive the fire. If it burns, let it become ash. If it endures, you may keep it but never stop testing it.

You may add to the fire of knowledge. But never believe you added all you could to it. 

Do not believe your own fire of knowledge is the bonfire of knowledge. 

No flame is sacred. Every flame may be examined.

Feed the flame not the ashes.

# Ritual 

Take lessons from the Phoenix as it dies and transforms into something new.  Use the winter solstice as a day of fasting and reflection. Write down who you are, what are your wants and goals, your desires. On this day eat nothing but eggs or seeds. On the summer solstice as a day of joy and renewal. Burn up your old writing of who you were, celebrate who you are. Pay no mind to who you were.  End your day by eating spicy poultry or spicy tofu. Feed the flame, not the ashes.
